# Disney's Magical Mirror

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains the options
for configuring and exporting a player file. For a local APWorld installation,
use **Generate Template Options** in Archipelago Launcher.

Follow the [setup guide](/tutorial/Disney%27s%20Magical%20Mirror/setup/en) to install the APWorld, patch your game,
and connect to a multiworld.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Items and rewards throughout the mansion are shuffled. An item may help you or
another player in your multiworld, and your own progression items may be found
in someone else's game.

Tricks can require unlock items, with optional shuffled or randomized star
costs. Some unlocks cover multiple stages of a trick. Consecutive tricks still
require enough star power for the complete sequence.

After visiting Mirror Room during normal gameplay, hold **L + R + Y** for one
second to return there from another room. Fully press both triggers. Mickey
must have normal control; the shortcut cannot interrupt tricks, cutscenes,
loading, or saving.

You can also choose an Organization XIII, Steamboat Willie, or
Sorcerer costume. Costumes only change Mickey's appearance.

## What is the goal of Disney's Magical Mirror when randomized?

Collect the required Mirror Shards and escape through the mirror to return
Mickey home. The required count is configurable from 1 to 12 and defaults to 12.
Souvenir checks that require all twelve shards keep that requirement even with
a lower goal count.

## What items and locations can get shuffled?

Locations include:

- Mirror Shard, Star Container, key, and quest-item pickups.
- Star pickups.
- All 23 souvenir rewards.
- 36 trick checks.
- 30 Hidden Hat boxes.

Items that can be shuffled include:

- Mirror Shards and Star Containers.
- Door-specific keys.
- Quest items such as Coins, Marble, Gear, Flashlight, and Magnifying Glass.
- 33 trick unlock items, when Tricks is enabled.
- Star Refills as filler.

## What does another world's item look like in Disney's Magical Mirror?

Pickups with a randomized item display show the Archipelago logo for another
world's item.

## When the player receives an item, what happens?

The client delivers items when Mickey has normal control. Inventory items are
added to the inventory, while keys, tricks, shards, and containers update their
corresponding game state. Delivery waits during menus, tricks, and cutscenes.
Reconnecting does not give already received items again.
